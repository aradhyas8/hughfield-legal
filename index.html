Four screens, start to sleep.

Hushfield asks one question, builds a room out of the answer, and hands you the sliders. That is the whole app.

  1. 1.0

    Say how you want to feel.

    Sleep better, focus, unwind, or mask unwanted noise. Four answers, and no sign-up screen between you and the first sound.

  2. 2.0

    It builds the atmosphere.

    Soft Brown Noise under the steady hum of a box fan — a warm fan shield, assembled from what you said. Rename it, keep it, or choose different sounds.

  3. 3.0

    Then take the sliders.

    Rain on the Roof at 62 %. Firelight at 38 %. An air conditioner holding the floor at 72 %. Move one and the room moves with it.

  4. 4.0

    Come back at one in the morning.

    Still awake? Hushfield picks up the atmosphere you left running and puts the short way back to sleep at the top of the screen.
