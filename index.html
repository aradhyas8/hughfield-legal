Four screens to somewhere quieter.

Choose what you need, find a place, adjust its sounds, and return whenever you need a quieter moment.

  1. 1.0

    Choose what you need.

    Sleep, focus, relax, mask noise, or let Hushfield surprise you. Pick a direction and start listening.

  2. 2.0

    Find your place.

    Step into Corner Table, Rain Outside: café murmur, rain against the windows, and soft ventilation in one place.

  3. 3.0

    Make it yours.

    Turn down the café or bring the rain closer. Make quick changes, adjust the sound levels, and save a version of the place you like.

  4. 4.0

    Come back at one in the morning.

    Still awake? Hushfield puts your last place at the top of Home, ready to play again.
