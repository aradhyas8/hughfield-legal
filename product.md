# Hushfield

Hushfield is an ambient sound app for iPhone and Android. It provides immersive atmospheres for sleep, focus, relaxation, and noise masking.

## Core experience

- Browse and play ambient sounds and atmospheres.
- Blend sound layers into personal mixes.
- Use a sleep timer to fade audio away gradually.
- Receive on-device personalized sound and mix suggestions based on how the app is used.
